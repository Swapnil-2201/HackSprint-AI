# 🚀 HackSprint AI – Your AI Hackathon Mentor

> **An AI-powered mentor that helps students and developers transform ideas into hackathon-ready projects with intelligent guidance, structured roadmaps, and presentation support.**

🌐 **Live Demo:** https://hack-sprint-ai-psi.vercel.app/

---

## 📌 Overview

HackSprint AI is an intelligent hackathon companion designed to guide participants from **idea generation to final project submission**.

Instead of spending hours deciding what to build or how to begin, users can interact with an AI mentor that provides project guidance, technology recommendations, development roadmaps, and presentation assistance—all through an intuitive web interface.

Whether you're a beginner entering your first hackathon or an experienced developer looking to accelerate planning, HackSprint AI acts as your virtual mentor throughout the journey.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Hackathons are fast-paced, competitive, and time-constrained.

Many participants struggle with:

* Choosing the right project idea
* Planning the overall architecture
* Selecting an appropriate technology stack
* Dividing work among team members
* Preparing a convincing final presentation
* Access to experienced mentors

These challenges often reduce productivity and impact project quality.

---

## 💡 Solution

HackSprint AI provides an AI-driven mentorship experience that assists teams throughout the hackathon lifecycle.

It helps users:

* Refine ideas
* Plan projects
* Recommend technologies
* Break projects into manageable tasks
* Guide implementation
* Prepare final presentations

The platform minimizes planning time so teams can focus on building.
